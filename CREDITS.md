# Credits

## Jason AI and Offline Bots Mod

The primary inspiration for ResurrectedOfflineBots was the anonymous **Jason AI and Offline Bots Mod** made for the original Friday the 13th: The Game.

That project demonstrated that an autonomous Jason could work and strongly influenced the direction of this Resurrected implementation. Thank you to its anonymous creator.
